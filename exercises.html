Mental Wellness Exercises

These exercises support emotional balance, stress recovery, focus improvement, and mindfulness through psychologically calming wellness activities.

🌬 Deep Breathing

Calms the nervous system and reduces stress naturally.

  • Inhale slowly for 4 seconds
  • Hold for 4 seconds
  • Exhale for 6 seconds
  • Repeat calmly
2–5 minutes

Helps calm anxious thoughts and reconnect attention.
